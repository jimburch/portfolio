Same motion, timing, fingers, thumb and lanyard as v9. What's moving: the wearer's right hand comes up from below, closes into a fist on the badge's bottom-right corner (thumb on the front beside the bottom keys, fingertips hooked round the edge, the screen left clear) and lifts. While the front faces away it slides into a phone grip: palm behind the card, all four fingertips curled over one long edge onto the face (index highest and a little apart, little finger lowest and shortest), the thumb pad pressing the face at the other edge, the heel and a short bare wrist below the card, then the cuff and the sleeve going back toward the body (wrist 10° ulnar, 17° extension).
Judge: the wrist between the card's bottom edge and the cuff: its width against the fingers, whether both contours run smoothly from the heel to the cuff, and whether the hand and forearm read as one limb, in the held pose and mid-turn.
