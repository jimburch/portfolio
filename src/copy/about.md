Hello, I'm Jim, a software developer at Showit. I live in Mesa, AZ, and I grew up near St. Louis.

I took an unusual path to software development. After 10 years in creative marketing, I was laid off during the pandemic. In between applications and job searching, I spent my free time learning to code and decided to officially change careers. I went through Galvanize's Advanced Software Engineering Immersive in 2021 and left marketing behind for good.

My programming career started at FanPower, a sports data company, where I was a full-stack developer. Since March 2024 I've been at Showit on the Applications team, working on the Design App, the platform where users design, publish, and manage their websites.
